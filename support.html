Email cosmoplasty@gmail.com. Replies come from TheDevAnt, the person who made the game.

Reporting a bug? Include your device, the app version, and the TURN and SEED shown on the end-of-run screen. The same seed always plays the same, so with those two numbers your run can be replayed exactly.

How to play

The same battle runs in three lanes, the timelines. Tap an enemy in any lane to strike it. The strike lands in full in that lane and echoes down the same column in the other two, so enemies lined up in one column all take a hit at once. The lane you tap holds still for that turn; the other two advance. An enemy that reaches the gate on the left takes one heart from that lane. A lane with no hearts left collapses and the run ends; tap to start the next one.

  • Small enemies (triangles) die to a single echo.
  • Big ones (hexagons) take a full hit and then one more. The pips under them show what is left.
  • The lane you strike holds still: use it to line enemies up, then take the whole column with one tap.

Settings

Tap the gear at the top right, or use the end-of-run screen. Four toggles: sound, haptics, screen shake, and reduced motion (fewer particles, no hit-stop, no shake; it follows your system setting on first launch). The pause screen also has New run.

Common questions

No sound on iPhone?
Check the ring/silent switch. In this version the silent switch mutes the game's audio, like a web page.
The hints disappeared.
The five one-line hints show during your first run only. Reinstalling the app, or clearing its data, brings them back.
Does the game save my run?
Yes. If you leave mid-run, the app resumes at the same turn, score and hearts when you come back, with a RESUMED tag.
Does it need an account, a connection, or collect anything?
No account, works fully offline, collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
Is the enemy timing random?
Spawns are paced, not rolled: the schedule is fixed for a given seed, so luck never decides a run. The seed is shown on the end-of-run screen.
