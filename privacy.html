Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026
Developer: TheDevAnt, cosmoplasty@gmail.com

Summary

Chronopath has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a record of each finished run, sent to the game's leaderboard while the Ranking setting is on, together with a random code the app makes for your device so the leaderboard can show your best once. The leaderboard shows you under a made-up tag such as NEON-WARDEN-07, never your name. With Ranking off, the app makes no network connections at all.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a few things on your device so it can work: your settings (sound, haptics, screen shake, reduced motion, ranking, light or dark theme), your best score, your best streak, your longest run, how many runs you have played, the run in progress so it can resume after you leave the app, whether you have seen the first-run hints, which tip comes next, your chosen art style, and, on slower phones, whether the lighter picture is needed (with the browser version it was measured on). It also keeps your progress: today's goals and how far you are with them, the medals you have earned, totals across your runs, your recent scores and the day's benchmark score, your rank, the looks you have unlocked and wear, and today's time capsule. For the leaderboard it keeps your best score on each board, the last leaderboard you looked at (so it can be shown offline), up to five finished runs waiting to be sent while you are offline, your device's random leaderboard code and your tag. None of this leaves your device except as described below, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. A run card you share goes only where you send it.

Leaderboard (optional, on by default)

When a run ends and Ranking is on, the app sends the run to our leaderboard server: its seed (the number that sets up the level), the list of taps you made, which board it is for (all-time or daily) and, for a daily run, the date. With it goes a random code the app made on your device the first time it sent a run. The code is not your device's hardware or advertising identifier and has nothing to do with your name, any account or any other app. The server replays your taps with the game's own rules to confirm the score. It keeps a one-way fingerprint of your code (not the code itself), a tag made from that fingerprint (two words and two numbers, such as NEON-WARDEN-07), and, for each board, your best score with its number of turns, the board and date, the seed, the time it arrived and a one-way fingerprint of the run so the same run is never counted twice. The taps themselves are not kept. The leaderboard shows the top players and the players around you by their tags, with their scores and turns.

The server never receives your name, an account, your location or your contacts. Your tag is the same each time you play on this device, so your entries can be linked to each other, but not to you: we cannot tell who is behind a tag. If you would like your entries removed, email us your tag (shown on the leaderboard screen as “YOU ARE …”). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the code, and the app makes a new one, with a new tag, next time.

Like any internet service, the server sees your device's IP address when the app connects. It is used only for a moment, to limit how often runs can be sent, and is never stored or logged by us.

  • Where: Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1, run by Cloudflare, Inc. on our behalf on its global network.
  • How long: daily boards are deleted after 30 days. All-time boards are kept for the current season; a new season starts whenever the game's rules change. Run fingerprints are deleted after 90 days.
  • Turning it off: set Ranking to OFF in SETTINGS on the pause or end-of-run screen. Nothing is sent after that, and runs waiting to be sent are discarded.

Data we collect

Only the leaderboard records described above: your best scores, linked to a random code's fingerprint and its tag. We do not know who you are, and we do not use the records for anything but the leaderboard.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. It is built with Capacitor, an open-source wrapper that does not collect data. The leaderboard server is hosted by Cloudflare as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The leaderboard never shows names or anything a player typed, only made-up tags, scores and turns.

Changes

If a future version changes what is sent, this policy and the store listings will be updated first.
