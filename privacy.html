Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026
Developer: TheDevAnt, cosmoplasty@gmail.com

Summary

Chronopath does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The only thing that ever leaves your device is an anonymous record of each finished run, sent to the game's leaderboard while the Ranking setting is on. It contains nothing that identifies you. With Ranking off, the app makes no network connections at all.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a few things on your device so it can work: your settings (sound, haptics, screen shake, reduced motion, ranking), your best score, and the run in progress so it can resume after you leave the app. For the leaderboard it also keeps your best score on each board, the last leaderboard you looked at (so it can be shown offline), up to five finished runs waiting to be sent while you are offline, and short random codes for your runs that reached a top 100 (so the board can mark them “YOU”). None of this leaves your device except as described below, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Leaderboard (optional, on by default)

When a run ends and Ranking is on, the app sends the run to our leaderboard server: its seed (the number that sets up the level), the list of taps you made, which board it is for (all-time or daily) and, for a daily run, the date. The server replays your taps with the game's own rules to confirm the score. It then keeps only the score, the number of turns, the board and date, the seed, the time it arrived, and a one-way fingerprint of the run so the same run is never counted twice. The taps themselves are not kept.

The server never receives your name, an account, a device identifier, your location or your contacts. Leaderboard entries are anonymous: they cannot be linked to you or to one another, which is also why we cannot find or delete “your” entries on request.

Like any internet service, the server sees your device's IP address when the app connects. It is used only for a moment, to limit how often runs can be sent, and is never stored or logged by us.

  • Where: Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1, run by Cloudflare, Inc. on our behalf on its global network.
  • How long: daily boards are deleted after 30 days. All-time boards are kept for the current season; a new season starts whenever the game's rules change. Run fingerprints are deleted after 90 days.
  • Turning it off: set Ranking to OFF on the pause or end-of-run screen. Nothing is sent after that, and runs waiting to be sent are discarded.

Data we collect

No personal data. The anonymous leaderboard records described above are the only data that reach us.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. It is built with Capacitor, an open-source wrapper that does not collect data. The leaderboard server is hosted by Cloudflare as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The leaderboard never shows names or any information about a player, only scores, turns and times.

Changes

If a future version changes what is sent, this policy and the store listings will be updated first.
