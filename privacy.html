Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026
Developer: TheDevAnt, cosmoplasty@gmail.com

Summary

Chronopath does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections.

Data stored on your device

The game saves three things locally on your device so it can work: your settings (sound, haptics, screen shake, reduced motion), your best score, and the state of a run in progress so it can resume after you leave the app. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, usage data, crash reports or any other information.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking. It is built with Capacitor, an open-source wrapper that does not collect data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds any data collection (for example, optional crash reporting), this policy and the store listings will be updated first, and the app will ask before anything is sent.
